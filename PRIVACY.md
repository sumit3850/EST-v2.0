# Privacy Policy — Entomological Surveillance Tool (EST)

The current, binding privacy policy is published at
**https://sumit3850.github.io/EST-v2.0/privacy.html** (also bundled inside the
Android / iOS apps as `privacy.html`). Terms of use: `terms.html`.

Summary (the web page is authoritative):

- **Who**: Dr. B. Sumit Kumar Rao, State Entomologist, NVBDCP – State Health Society, Andaman & Nicobar Islands.
- **Data**: account details (username, name, email, mobile, hashed password, role/approval), survey data you enter (dates, places, field staff names, mosquito counts, indices), GPS coordinates and survey tracks captured only when you start a survey or tap a GPS button, optional breeding-site photos, payment references (never card/UPI details), sign-in and recovery logs. No ads, analytics or tracking SDKs.

- **Retention**: survey records are programme records; account data lives while the account exists; logs up to 12 months.
- **Delete your account**: in the app — Home → *Delete my account* (type DELETE) — or email explorer3850@gmail.com from your registered address. Surveys remain as programme records, unlinked from you.
- **Contact**: explorer3850@gmail.com · WhatsApp +91 95318 06405.
