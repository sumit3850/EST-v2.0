1. Who we are

EST is a professional tool for mosquito-vector surveillance developed and operated by Dr. B. Sumit Kumar Rao, State Entomologist, National Vector Borne Disease Control Programme (NVBDCP), State Health Society, Andaman & Nicobar Islands, India ("we", "us"). It is intended for public-health field staff, entomological teams and authorised surveillance stations — not for the general public and not for children.

2. Data we collect

2.1 Account information

  • Username, first and last name, email address, mobile number, password (stored only as a one-way hash by our authentication provider). Email and mobile number are used to sign in, to send one-time codes for password or username recovery, and for the administrator to contact you about your account.
  • Role and approval status (field user / administrator; pending / approved) set by the EST administrator.

2.2 Survey data you enter

  • Survey date, State/UT, district, zone/region, locality, names of field workers and officers present.
  • Entomological counts and observations: mosquito species and numbers, houses and containers inspected, breeding sites, dipping results, remarks.
  • Derived indices (House, Container, Breteau, Pupal and density indices) and risk categories.

2.3 Location data

  • GPS coordinates of the survey start point and of each house/site you tag, and the path recorded while a survey track is running. Location is captured only when you start a survey or tap a GPS button, never in the background.
  • On the mobile app, the operating system asks for location permission at first start; you can withdraw it any time in the device settings (surveys then save without coordinates).

2.4 Photos

If you attach a photo of a breeding site, the camera or gallery is used only at that moment and the image is stored with that survey.

2.5 Payment information

Pro subscriptions are processed by Razorpay (website), Google Play Billing (Android app) or Apple In-App Purchase (iOS app). We never see or store card, UPI or bank details — we receive only a payment or transaction reference, the plan purchased and its validity period.

2.6 Technical data

  • Sign-in events (username, time, method) and account-recovery events, kept for security.
  • We do not use advertising identifiers, analytics or tracking SDKs, device fingerprinting or third-party cookies.

4. Where data is stored and how it is protected

  • On your device: surveys are first saved locally (offline-first) so you can work without a network. They stay on the device until you delete them or remove the app.
  • In the cloud: when you sync, surveys and account data are stored in a database hosted by Supabase Inc. on Amazon Web Services infrastructure. Transport is encrypted (HTTPS/TLS); data is encrypted at rest by the provider. Access is controlled per account: a field user can read and edit only their own records; administrators can read all records of the programme.
  • Passwords are never stored in readable form. One-time recovery codes expire after 10 minutes.
  • Access to the administrative dashboard is limited to approved administrator accounts.

5. Sharing

We do not sell personal data and do not share it with advertisers. Data is disclosed only to:

  • Service providers acting on our instructions: Supabase (database and authentication), Razorpay / Google / Apple (payments), the SMS and email providers used to deliver one-time codes.
  • Map tile providers when a map is shown or exported: Google Maps and Esri World Imagery receive the map coordinates requested, under their own privacy policies. No account data is sent with tile requests.
  • The surveillance programme: aggregated indices and reports (by district, region, locality) are used for official NVBDCP reporting. Reports may include survey localities and the names of field staff as recorded in the survey.
  • Authorities where required by law.

6. Retention

  • Survey records are programme records and are retained for as long as the surveillance programme needs them.
  • Account information is kept while your account exists and deleted when you delete the account (below). Sign-in and recovery logs are kept for up to 12 months.
  • Payment references are kept for the statutory accounting period.

7. Delete your account

In the app: Home → scroll down → Delete my account → confirm by typing DELETE. Available in the Android and iOS apps and on the website.

By request: email explorer3850@gmail.com from your registered address with your username. We action requests within 7 days.

Deleting the account removes your sign-in, profile (name, email, phone) and licences. Surveys you submitted remain in the programme database as surveillance records but are no longer linked to your account; data saved only on your device can be removed when you delete the account or by clearing the app's data.

8. Your rights

You can access and correct your profile from the Operations Console, export your own surveys (XLSX / PDF) from the app, and delete your account as described above. Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (India) and similar laws you may also ask us to correct or erase personal data, or raise a grievance, using the contact below.

9. Children

EST is a professional tool and is not directed at persons under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

10. Changes

We will post any changes on this page and update the effective date. Material changes are announced in the app.
